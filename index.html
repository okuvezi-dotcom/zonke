We work when you close.

All five lines in one night visit, so nobody sprays near your food and no truck sits in your bay while you trade. This is a typical night at a restaurant that closes at ten.

  1. 22:30

    You close

    We sign in with your night manager, or let ourselves in from the key safe.

  2. 22:45

    Pest

    Every bait station and monitor checked. Any activity is pinned on the trap map.

  3. 23:30

    Washroom

    Bins exchanged, dispensers filled, drains treated.

  4. 00:15

    Grease

    Trap level measured. On pump-out nights the licensed tanker meets us at the back door.

  5. 01:00

    Clean

    This month's deep-clean zone, from the ceiling down to the floor drains.

  6. 03:30

    Air

    Canopy filters swapped. Each quarter, every aircon unit gets its service.

  7. 04:30

    Lock up

    We punch the tag on the back door. Photos and certificates go onto your Door Record.

  8. 07:00

    You open

    To a kitchen that would pass if the inspector walked in today.

The Door Record

The inspector comes in through the back door. So do we.

Every Zonke site gets a tag that hangs inside the back door. At every visit we punch the month on each line we serviced.

The QR code on the tag opens the site's full record: who came, what they did, what they found, and every certificate. Your Environmental Health Practitioner, insurer, franchisor or landlord can check it without phoning you, and you never have to search for a docket.

The Zonke terms.

The things people complain about most in this industry, written into the contract so you never have to.

  1. No lock-in

    Cancel any time with 30 days' notice. No cancellation fee, and no one calling you to talk you out of it.

  2. One person, on WhatsApp

    A named account manager for your site, who replies within 2 working hours.

  3. Re-service guarantee

    If an inspector flags something on a line we cover, we come back within 48 hours and put it right at no charge.

  4. Licensed where the law says so

    Pest work is done by or under a registered pest control operator (Act 36). Refrigerant work is done by SAQCC Gas-registered practitioners.

  5. Products on file

    Disinfectants with an NRCS letter of authority, and a safety data sheet for every chemical we use on your site.

  6. One price, fixed

    A free survey, then one monthly price per site for all five lines. It changes only if the scope does.
