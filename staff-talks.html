Add-on · Staff talks

Ten minutes at shift change.

Five short talks for kitchen staff, each with one question to ask at the end and a sheet for everyone to sign. Print one a week. Zonke clients get them given by our technicians, with the signed sheet filed on the Door Record.

01
Staff talk · 10 minutes

Wash your hands, every time

  1. Wash before you start, after the toilet, after bins or raw meat, after touching your face or phone, and after a break.
  2. Wet, soap, then rub for 20 seconds: backs of hands, between fingers, under nails, thumbs.
  3. Rinse, then dry with a paper towel. Turn the tap off with the towel.
  4. Use the hand basin, never the prep sink. Tell the manager when soap or towels run out.

Ask at the endShow me the hand basin. Name three times today you should have washed your hands.

02
Staff talk · 10 minutes

Check every delivery for pests

  1. Check boxes and crates before they come inside: droppings, gnaw marks, live insects, webbing in dry goods.
  2. Unpack cardboard at the door and take it straight out. Cockroaches hide and lay eggs in it.
  3. Set aside anything with signs, and tell the manager and Zonke the same day.
  4. Keep the back door shut between deliveries.

Ask at the endWhat three things do you look for when a delivery arrives?

03
Staff talk · 10 minutes

Close down so pests find nothing

  1. Clear food and crumbs from under and behind equipment, not just the tops.
  2. Keep dry goods in sealed tubs, off the floor.
  3. Empty and close the bins, wipe up spills, and dry the dish area and floor drains. Pests follow water.
  4. Shut the doors and windows. No food is left out overnight.

Ask at the endWhat is the last thing you check before you lock up?

04
Staff talk · 10 minutes

Spot it, report it

  1. Look for droppings like ground pepper (cockroaches), droppings like rice (mice), gnawed packaging, grease marks along walls, and insects out in daylight.
  2. Don't spray it yourself. Sprays scatter cockroaches into new hiding places and can get onto food.
  3. Tell the manager in the same shift: what you saw, where and when. Take a photo if you can.
  4. The manager reports it to Zonke on WhatsApp or on the report page, and we decide how fast to come.

Ask at the endIf you saw a cockroach today, who would you tell, and what would you tell them?

05
Staff talk · 10 minutes

Cleaning chemicals, safely

  1. Read the label and the safety data sheet. Mix to the strength it says, not stronger.
  2. Never mix two chemicals. Some mixtures, like bleach with acid or ammonia cleaners, give off a poisonous gas.
  3. Keep chemicals in their own labelled containers, stored away from food.
  4. Wear the gloves and eye protection the label asks for. If it splashes on skin or in eyes, rinse with water and tell the manager.

Ask at the endWhere are the safety data sheets kept, and which chemical here must never be mixed with another?
