POPIA

Privacy notice.

How Zonke Hygiene (Pty) Ltd ("Zonke") collects, uses and protects personal information under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

What we collect

When you contact us: your name, business, phone number, email address and area, and whatever you tell us about your premises. When we service your site: the names of the people who sign us in and out, photos of the areas we work on, and records of what we found and did.

What this website collects

Nothing, unless you send it. The back-door check and the spend check run in your browser, and the check remembers your answers only in your browser. The survey booking, sighting report and survey forms open WhatsApp or your email app with your message filled in, and you press send. This website uses no tracking cookies and no analytics.

Sighting photos and visit ratings

A photo you send with a sighting report is used to decide how fast to come and what to bring, and is kept with your site's service record. A visit rating and comment go to your account manager. We ask you not to photograph people.

Why we use it

To quote, schedule and carry out services; to keep the service records that our clients, their inspectors and their insurers rely on; to invoice; and to meet our legal obligations. We don't sell personal information and we don't use it for anyone else's marketing.

The Door Record

Each site's record is reached through the QR code on its back-door tag. It shows service details and technician registrations, not customers' personal information. Clients choose who gets the link.

Who we share it with

Only the partners who do part of the work on your site (for example the licensed waste carrier or the SAQCC Gas-registered technician), our accountants, and authorities where the law requires it. Each is bound to keep it confidential.

How long we keep it

Service records for as long as you are a client and for five years after, because inspectors and insurers may ask for them. Enquiries that don't become a contract, for one year.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how we use it. Email hello@callzonke.co.za. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

Information officer

The information officer is the managing director of Zonke Hygiene (Pty) Ltd, reachable at hello@callzonke.co.za.
